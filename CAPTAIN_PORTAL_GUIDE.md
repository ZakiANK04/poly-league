# Poly League Captain Portal Guide

## Purpose

The Captain Portal is the private workspace for the eight department captains. Each captain can manage only the tournament content assigned to their account. Public visitors do not see a portal link; use the confidential portal URL supplied by the tournament administrator.

Portal URL: `/captain-portal`

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the private portal URL.
2. Enter the email and password associated with your captain account.
3. If the account is authenticated but no team is shown, contact the tournament administrator to link the account to your department.
4. Use **Sign Out** whenever you finish on a shared device.

## 2. Register your squad

1. Open the **Squad Registration** tab.
2. Enter the player's full name.
3. Enter a kit number from 1 to 99.
4. Choose the player's position: goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, or forward.
5. Select **Register to Squad**.
6. Confirm that the player appears in the squad list and on your public team page.

Check spelling and shirt numbers before submitting. Duplicate registrations should be avoided.

## 3. Manage match scores

1. Open **Schedule & Match Scores**.
2. Find a fixture involving your team.
3. Enter or update the score and match status using the fixture controls.
4. Save the update and confirm the public fixtures and standings reflect the result.

Only publish a score after the match has been completed and agreed by the participating captains. Keep the official result consistent with the match report.

## 4. Run the league draw

1. Open **League Draw**.
2. Confirm that all teams and fixtures are ready.
3. Run the draw once the captains agree that the schedule can be published.
4. Wait for the completion confirmation before leaving the page.

Do not repeatedly run the draw. Use **Reset Demo** only during local demonstration or testing; it clears locally stored demo changes.

## 5. Run the knockout draw

1. Open **Official Knockout Draw**.
2. Confirm the league standings and qualification positions.
3. Start the draw when the league phase is complete.
4. Wait for the draw animation and completion message.
5. Check the public bracket before announcing the matchups.

A completed draw should be treated as official. Reset it only when the captains have agreed that a correction is required.

## 6. Publish news and highlights

1. Open **News & Highlights**.
2. Choose the content type: score, article, or video.
3. Add a clear title and accurate description.
4. Add the media URL when applicable.
5. For a score item, enter the final scoreline.
6. Select the publish action and review the public Highlights page.
7. Use the edit or remove controls to correct an existing item.

Use concise titles, verified scores, and media that the tournament is allowed to publish.

## 7. Good operating practice

- Review every change on the public page after publishing.
- Use one captain account per department.
- Never share passwords or leave a signed-in session unattended.
- Record score changes in the agreed captain group before publishing.
- Avoid Reset Demo in production.
- Report access problems with the account email, department, and time of the issue. Never send a password.

## Quick checklist

Before finishing a session:

- Squad names and numbers are correct.
- Fixture scores and statuses are verified.
- Draw results are published only after captain approval.
- Highlights have accurate titles, descriptions, and media.
- Public pages display the intended updates.
- You have signed out.

## Support

The tournament administrator manages account linking and Supabase access. Captains manage their own squad, fixtures, and approved tournament updates through the portal.
