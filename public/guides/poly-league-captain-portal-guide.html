Captain Portal
Field Guide

A practical handbook for the eight department captains who manage squads, fixtures, draws, and approved tournament updates.

Private operations document
Use only with your assigned captain account. Version 1.0 · September 2026

01 · Access & responsibility

The Captain Portal is a private workspace. Public visitors do not see a portal link. Open the confidential URL supplied by the tournament administrator, sign in with your assigned account, and use Sign Out on shared devices.

Access rule: each account is linked to one department. If the portal opens without your team profile, stop and contact the administrator. Never share passwords.

02 · Squad registration

1. Open Squad Registration

Confirm the team badge and department shown in the captain header.

2. Enter player details

Use the player's full name, a kit number from 1–99, and the correct position.

3. Register once

Select Register to Squad and wait for the success confirmation.

4. Verify publicly

Check the squad list and your public team page. Remove duplicates or mistakes before matchday.

03 · Fixtures & scores

  1. Open Schedule & Match Scores and locate a fixture involving your department.
  2. Enter the agreed score and match status, then save the update.
  3. Confirm the public Fixtures page and Standings page show the intended result.
Before publishing: only enter a final score after both participating captains agree with the official match report.

04 · Draw controls

League Draw

Run it only after the captains agree the schedule is ready. Wait for the completion state and do not repeat the action.

Official Knockout Draw

Confirm qualification positions, start once the league phase is complete, wait for the animation, then verify the public bracket.

Reset Demo is for local demonstration only. A completed draw should be treated as official unless the captains approve a correction.

05 · News & highlights

  1. Open News & Highlights and choose score, article, or video.
  2. Add a concise title and accurate description. Add a permitted media URL when needed.
  3. For score items, enter the final scoreline.
  4. Publish, then review the public Highlights page. Use edit or remove to correct an item.

06 · Session close checklist

  • Names and kit numbers checked
  • Scores agreed and verified
  • Draws run only with approval
  • Highlights accurately titled
  • Public pages reviewed
  • Shared device signed out

Support

For account linking or access problems, contact the tournament administrator with your account email, department, and the time of the issue. Do not send a password.
